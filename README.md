# Supply Chain KPI & Reporting Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain and operational performance through an interactive Excel dashboard focused on profitability, supplier metrics, inventory trends, and KPI monitoring.

## Objectives

- Monitor supplier and SKU performance
- Track profitability and operational trends
- Analyze lead times and defect rates
- Support reporting and KPI monitoring

## Key Metrics

- Total Tracked Profit: ₱2.28M+
- Analyzed SKUs: 100
- Monitor Strategy Contribution: ₱1.17M

## Analysis Areas

- Profit by action
- Risk tolerance and profitability
- Cost vs. profit analysis
- Lead time vs. shipping time
- Average defect rate by supplier
- SKU volume by supplier
- Supplier and SKU performance

## Key Findings

- The Monitor action generated the highest profit contribution at approximately ₱1.17M.
- The Scale action contributed approximately ₱856K and showed strong growth potential.
- Lead times exceeded shipping times in the analyzed data.
- Supplier 1 had the lowest average defect rate.
- Higher-cost SKUs generally produced higher profits.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PivotTables
- Excel dashboarding
- Data visualization
- KPI reporting
